Zavicefta Renal Dosing

Recommended Dosage
Dialyzable: 57% (Administer after Dialysis)
Infusion Time: Over 2 Hours
Stability: 24 Hours at 2 - 8°C (Reconstituted)
Monitoring: Prophylaxis (1st dose) & Renal Function
